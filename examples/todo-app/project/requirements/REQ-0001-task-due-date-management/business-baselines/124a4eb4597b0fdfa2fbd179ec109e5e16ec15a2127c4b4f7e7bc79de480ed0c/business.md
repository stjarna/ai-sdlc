# Business Refinement — REQ-0001

Source revision: f721697ed58c51c59ad8e37bb7c62bea6612cd4f003ee4fd7b5e333393f22f8d

## Confirmed information
### Business need and intended value
- **B-001** [D-0001] Users need a simple way to record due dates for their tasks so they can better keep track of upcoming commitments.

### Initial scope
- **B-002** [D-0001, D-0003, D-0005, D-0004] Users should be able to assign an optional due date to each task. Reminders and notifications remain outside the initial scope.
- **B-003** [D-0005, D-0004] Deadlines are optional. The primary evidence is the explicit human confirmation D-0005; D-0004 is the originating AI assumption.

### Exclusions
- **B-004** [D-0003] Reminders are outside the initial scope.

## Unresolved issues
- **U-001** [D-0006] Deferred: whether users may choose a date in the past. Decision D-0007 defers this question until consultation with support. No rule allowing or rejecting past dates has been decided.

## Unconfirmed proposals
None recorded.
