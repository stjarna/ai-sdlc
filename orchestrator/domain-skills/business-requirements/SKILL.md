# Business Requirements domain skill

Use this package to turn completed Discovery into a clear, nontechnical business
specification. The Product Owner approves the exact business baseline through the
orchestrator. This package cannot approve, advance, assign or write governance metadata.

## Establish business authority

Derive confirmed content from current user statements, substantive human decisions and
explicitly confirmed assumptions, following corrections and resolution chains. Keep
unknown/deferred questions and unconfirmed proposals visibly separate. Original idea
prose provides context, not substitute confirmation. An acknowledgement of uncertainty
does not settle an open rule. Do not invent decisions, priorities or acceptance promises.

## Explain the intended change

Cover objectives/value, stakeholders and affected users, scope/exclusions, relevant
business processes/rules, constraints/dependencies, priorities and business acceptance
expectations where actual evidence supports them. Omit unsupported detail or identify
the consequential question; do not force every possible topic into the specification.
Use business language and observable outcomes, avoiding technology/design prescriptions.
Distinguish confirmed needs from suggestions. Negative conditions, defaults and validation
restrictions are business choices too: absence of a requirement is not authority to
reject the behavior. Prefer clarification over a prototype exception.

Preserve stable business IDs for the same requirement and trace each item to the actual
Discovery evidence. Cover all current source records in the correct category. Do not
renumber items for presentation or silently reorder their meaning. Template structure and
the existing validator define the exact supported item/reference syntax; do not invent
a second schema here. Business context and exclusions need no artificial functional IDs.

## Review and refinement

Review source alignment, understandable business meaning, consistency, scope boundaries
and open decisions. Explain what changed and what remains uncertain. A semantic review
is AI-assisted judgment; passing structural checks does not establish correctness or
approval. Preserve unchanged confirmed context instead of asking the person to repeat it.
A wording request is not permission to change meaning. New substantive requirements or
resolution of inherited business questions require the coordinator's governed source
reconciliation, not silently selected rules in this draft.

## Template and persistence

Resolve `sdlc.py templates business` and use the returned pinned scaffold. The template
owns document sections; domain guidance supplies content quality. The existing validators
own IDs, source coverage and structural rules. Persist through the coordinator's
business generation/revision operation, which records exact template and domain pins.
Return a concise value/scope/exclusions/uncertainty review for the Product Owner's explicit
decision. Approval and separate advancement, actual human wording and immutable baselines
remain orchestrator responsibilities. Legacy artifacts gain no retrospective skill pin.
